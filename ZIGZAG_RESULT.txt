STEP 4 — fixes aimed at the causes above, each measured on BOTH years
====================================================================================
The review says most losers are 'price went the other way' — the
confirmed reversal simply did not hold. So the fixes target that:
  v1 plain      trade the confirmed reversal
  v2 stop       cap how far a failed reversal can run
  v3 +minleg    skip signals following a leg too small to trust
  v5 INVERTED   if reversals fail this often, trade the continuation

The column that decides everything is GROSS — net with the fee added
back. Gross is the edge itself. If gross is not above the 0.110% fee,
no amount of tuning the exits can make the rule pay.
